# Meta Ads Audit Report Template

## Scoring Methodology

Score each dimension independently on its weighted scale. Sum for the final 0-100 score.

### Creative Health (25 points)
- 20-25: Less than 10% wasted spend, diverse creative mix
- 15-19: 10-20% wasted spend, moderate diversity
- 10-14: 20-35% wasted spend, limited variety
- 0-9: Over 35% wasted spend, heavy concentration

### Audience Efficiency (20 points)
- 16-20: No significant overlap, distinct segments, low frequency
- 11-15: Minor overlap, moderate frequency
- 6-10: Significant overlap, high frequency
- 0-5: Severe cannibalization, frequency above 4.0

### Budget Allocation (20 points)
- 16-20: Budget matches conversion share
- 11-15: Minor misallocation
- 6-10: Significant misallocation (30%+ in underperformers)
- 0-5: Severe misallocation

### Funnel Performance (15 points)
- 12-15: CTR above 1.5%, CVR above 3%
- 8-11: CTR 1.0-1.5%, CVR 1.5-3%
- 4-7: CTR below 1.0% or CVR below 1.5%
- 0-3: Fundamental funnel issues

### Fatigue Signals (10 points)
- 8-10: No fatigue patterns
- 5-7: 1-3 ads with early fatigue
- 2-4: Multiple ads with advanced fatigue
- 0-1: Account-wide fatigue

### Offer Effectiveness (10 points)
- 8-10: Clear offer-to-CPA relationship
- 5-7: Some offers outperform, no clear pattern
- 2-4: Random offer performance
- 0-1: No offer testing visible

## Grade Scale
- 85-100: A (Healthy)
- 70-84: B (Good, targeted fixes)
- 55-69: C (Needs work)
- 40-54: D (Significant issues)
- 0-39: F (Critical)
