# Cut Templates

Compression rules and beat allocation per placement. Use this when generating cuts from the 6-beat architecture.

---

## 15s Meta Feed Cut

**Target:** Facebook/Instagram Feed, Reels primary placement
**Goal:** Maximum hook density. Every second earns the next.
**Primary lever:** Compression — gets the full offer in 15s

```
[0–2s]   Beat 1 — Hook       (2s)
[2–5s]   Beat 2 — Problem    (3s) — compressed to 1-2 lines
[5–9s]   Beat 3 — Solution   (4s) — demo shot takes priority
[9–12s]  Beat 4 — Offer      (3s) — flat lay flash + price
[12–15s] Beat 5 — Close      (3s) — risk reversal + deadline
```

**Rules for 15s:**
- Hook must work with sound OFF. First frame tells the whole story.
- Problem beat = maximum 2 sentences. Name the failure, nothing more.
- Solution beat gets the most screen time. One clean demo shot with voiceover.
- Offer flat lay must be fast but readable. Show the bundle, say the price.
- Close with guarantee FIRST, then date. Never naked discount urgency.
- No music-driven pacing. This cut should work without any audio.

## 22s Reels Extended Cut

**Target:** Instagram/Facebook Reels
**Goal:** Full demo with one expanded proof beat
**Primary lever:** Demonstration — earns credibility before asking for the close

```
[0–2s]   Beat 1 — Hook                  (2s)
[2–5s]   Beat 2 — Problem               (3s)
[5–12s]  Beat 3 — Solution + Proof      (7s) ← expanded
[12–16s] Beat 4 — Offer                 (4s)
[16–22s] Beat 5 — Close                 (6s) ← expanded risk reversal
```

**Rules for 22s:**
- Beat 3 gets 7 seconds — use it for two proof points (demo shot 1 + demo shot 2).
- Beat 5 gets 6 seconds — full guarantee sentence + seasonal close + "link in bio."
- Voiceover can run slightly longer. Still punchy — no filler.
- Same assets as the 15s cut, just longer clips of the same scenes.

## 39s TikTok Native Cut

**Target:** TikTok, Instagram Stories
**Goal:** Full storytelling arc with setup, depth beat, and organic-feeling close
**Primary lever:** Social proof + storytelling pacing

```
[0–2s]    Beat 1 — Hook                 (2s)
[2–6s]    Beat 2 — Problem              (4s) ← fuller setup
[6–14s]   Beat 3 — Solution + Proof     (8s) ← full demo
[14–20s]  Beat 6 — Depth Beat           (6s) ← new: shows use scenario/objection pre-empt
[20–26s]  Beat 4 — Offer Reveal         (6s) ← named bonus reveal
[26–34s]  Beat 5 — Close                (8s) ← full risk reversal + story anchor
[34–39s]  Final CTA                     (5s)
```

**Depth Beat (Beat 6) options:**
- Usage scenario ("gets every side of every bar — the part most brushes miss")
- Objection pre-empt ("works on cast iron, stainless, porcelain")
- Social proof ("what I use before every cookout")
- Brand story moment ("built for backyard pitmasters, not commercial kitchens")

**Rules for 39s:**
- Pacing can breathe. TikTok attention curve is longer. Use it.
- Beat 6 solves the purchase objection that's NOT in the first 15s cut.
- Still uses same visual assets where possible. Depth Beat may need one additional scene.
- Voiceover can be more conversational. Less "ad copy" energy.
- Close should include 2-3 social proof signals (UGC feel if possible).
