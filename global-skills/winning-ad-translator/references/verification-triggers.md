# Verification Triggers

Claim categories that require `[VERIFY]` flags before any script goes to Meta. Per Hallucination Protocol.

Tag format: `[VERIFY] claim text — required before Meta submit`

---

## Category 1: Physical Claims (product capabilities)

Tag any claim about what the product physically does:
- Motor power / speed / stall resistance
- Cleaning time ("cleans in 5 minutes")
- Waterproof / water resistance ratings
- Temperature resistance
- Material safety (bristle-free, food-safe, etc.)
- Battery life / charge time
- Weight / dimensions (if not from confirmed spec sheet)

**Pit Smith specific:**
- `[VERIFY]` motor doesn't stall under grill pressure
- `[VERIFY]` exact cleaning time claim before stating it
- `[VERIFY]` "works on all grill types" — not confirmed for all grates
- `[VERIFY]` "dishwasher safe brush heads" — not in confirmed specs
- `[VERIFY]` "shed-proof bristles" — not confirmed in testing

**Palm Aura specific:**
- `[VERIFY]` heat temperature range
- `[VERIFY]` compression pressure specs
- `[VERIFY]` any claim about circulation, pain reduction, or recovery

---

## Category 2: Statistical / Survey Claims

Tag any percentage, count, or survey result:
- "X% of users reported..." — needs source
- "Used by X customers" — needs CRM verification
- "Cleans X% faster" — needs comparative test
- Star ratings / review counts — only use real data

**Rule:** Never invent a statistic. If the brand pack doesn't have a verified number, don't use one in the script. Say "pitmasters love this" not "9 out of 10 pitmasters prefer this."

---

## Category 3: Temporal Claims

Tag any date or deadline:
- Shipping cutoff dates ("ships for Father's Day by [DATE]")
- Sale end dates
- Limited stock availability

**Rule:** `[DATE]` is always a placeholder until the exact date is confirmed with the supplier / fulfillment team. Never hardcode a date in a script until confirmed.

---

## Category 4: Medical / Health Claims

Tag any claim that could be read as a health benefit:
- Pain relief ("relieves arthritis pain")
- Circulation ("improves blood circulation")
- Recovery ("speeds up muscle recovery")
- Any claim that resembles a drug/device claim under FTC / FDA rules

**Rule:** For Palm Aura / Abundria, frame as: "ritual", "relief", "feels calming" — not as therapeutic outcomes. Medical-adjacent claims require explicit legal review before Meta.

---

## Category 5: Competitor Comparisons

Tag any direct or implied comparison:
- "Better than [Competitor Brand]" — needs substantiation
- Comparison tables — any column that makes a factual claim needs verification
- Implied competitive superiority ("what pitmasters are switching to") — okay if framed as trend, not if stated as fact

---

## Pre-Submit Checklist

Before any script is approved for ad spend:

- [ ] All `[VERIFY]` flags resolved with confirmed source
- [ ] All `[DATE]` placeholders filled with real dates
- [ ] No medical/health claims unreviewed
- [ ] No competitor names used without legal review
- [ ] Compare-at price live in Shopify (not just in the script)
- [ ] Offer stack matches what's actually in the cart
- [ ] Coach review submitted (AI Com #store-reviews) ← required before scaling
