## **PHASE 7: OPTIMIZATION & SCALING**

### David Fogarty OS Methodology (5-day cycles)
1. **Days 1-3: Data Collection**
   - Let ads run
   - Monitor early signals
   - No changes

2. **Day 4: Analysis**
   - Identify winners (CTR > 2%, ROAS > 2.5x)
   - Identify losers (CTR < 1%, ROAS < 1.5x)

3. **Day 5: Kill/Iterate/Advance**
   - Kill: Turn off losers
   - Iterate: Modify underperformers
   - Advance: Scale winners (+20% budget)

### Optimization Actions
- Budget reallocation
- Ad creative refresh
- Audience expansion
- Placement adjustments
- Bid strategy changes

### Winning Ads Library (your "Winning Ads Library" view)
- PalmAura - Sleep Hook v1: 2.8% CTR, 4.1% CVR, 4.2x ROAS
- FlexiGrip - Morning Stiffness: 2.1% CTR, 3.4% CVR, 3.8x ROAS
- Avatar targets extracted

### Data to Track
