The products discussed in the video, ranked by their viability for testing based on the speaker's analysis, are:


Low Viability (Disapproved)
1. Suburb NAD+ (Supplement): Disapproved because it is a branded supplement, making it unsourceable, and with supplements, there is no quality control when sourcing randomly from AliExpress.
2. 5-in-1 Ceramic Curling Wand (Branded): Disapproved because it is a branded product ("Wavy Talk") and therefore not easily sourceable. Even similar generic versions are unlikely to match the quality and unique features of the branded product. High-level competitors are already successful with this product, making it non-viable.
3. Yagu Fitness (Treadmill): Disapproved because it is branded ("Yagood Fitness"), too large and bulky to ship, and involves complex technology, leading to potential logistics issues and complications in getting it to the customer in one piece.
4. Oil Sprayer (Oil Sprayer and Pourer): Disapproved, despite having good angles, because the price is too low, and it is not realistic to sell it at higher price points (like $40) because there are many variations and different brands selling similar products for $10-$20. Additionally, no actual drop shippers were seen running this product on Meta.
5. Marac Vibration Plate: Mentioned as a branded product that is too big and bulky, and has been submitted by many students and is run by high-level competitors.
Medium Viability (Needs More Research)
1. Happy Code Electric Teaser (Cat Toy): Recommended for more research to ensure it can be sourced and to see how competitors are performing. The "Happy Code" name is present, but it does not appear on the product itself, and the images look like they were pulled from AliExpress.
2. Body Massager Belt (Heating and Massager Belt): Given a "three" (needs more research) initially due to the potential for different angles and the need to check sourcing and competitors. However, a similar product was previously disapproved for another student.
High Viability (Approved/Potentially Good)
1. Hand Pouch Heating Pad (Heating Pad/Pouch): Mentioned as a product with "massive potential" but lacked validation and had poor reviews, making it not viable.
2. Wrist/Elbow Brace (New and Unique Variation): Considered "very interesting" due to its new and unique variation, but the price is a little high, and the speaker suggested looking into it more.
