The products that were approved or considered worth testing include:
* Rice Jam Mask The speaker liked the idea of this product, particularly because it seems to be aimed at brightening and removing dark spots, and it appears to be a potentially trendy product in that niche. While it is a branded product, it is not ingested and does not seem like a complex formula, making it the kind of product that AliExpress can often replicate.
* Heated Beard Straightener The speaker considered this product interesting and worth looking into, though it had low revenue on the platform ($5K in the last 30 days). Further validation was needed by checking its performance on Amazon (looking for 500 to 1,000+ sales in the last month and ratings of 4.2 stars and above) and checking for active ads on Facebook.
* Wax Warmer Kit While its revenue was low ($4K in the last 30 days), the speaker noted this product is definitely something that could be sourced and suggested looking into Amazon and Facebook for additional validation.
* Lotion Applicator The speaker considered this a "painful problem solver" and suggested there were great angles for targeting demographics like older people or those who lack flexibility due to issues like arthritis. Although this particular example was 100% custom-branded, the speaker liked the concept and suggested looking into it.
* Couch Cushion Support/Foam Cushions The speaker thought this was a "huge problem solver" because it addresses a very common issue and offers a quick, easy, and cheap solution (as it is literally just foam you throw under a cushion). The product also provides leverage for pricing, as customers are saving thousands compared to buying a new couch. More validation was still needed.
* UV Razor Disinfectant/Sanitizer This was introduced as a concept, drawing from UV toothbrush holders, with the speaker noting its potential as a cool, untapped product. Validation should be sought on Amazon and by checking for competitors on Facebook.
* Baby Playmat For those in the baby niche, the speaker found this product interesting and noted its potential, especially when angled towards solving the problem of hardwood floors. The product was starting to trend during the current month, and the speaker advised checking Amazon and looking for active competitors.
* Scalp Massager (interchangeable) While an older product, this version, which also works as a neck massager with swappable parts, was considered a strong product from the perspective of unique points of tension. The ability to swap the parts (unlike the older, strictly scalp version) provides many more angles and potential.
